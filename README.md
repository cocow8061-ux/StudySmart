# 🎓 StudySmart - AI-Powered Smart Study Planner

**Plan Smarter, Study Better!**  
StudySmart is a **responsive, user-friendly web application** designed to help students manage their studies, track progress, and stay focused using AI-powered scheduling.  

---

## ✨ Features

- 🗓 **Personalized Study Schedule**  
  Create your study plan by entering courses, assignments, and exam dates. Gemini API suggests the **optimal study schedule**.

- ⏱ **Focus Mode / Pomodoro Timer**  
  Stay productive with timers, breaks, and optional ambient sounds.

- 📈 **Progress Tracker & Analytics**  
  Track study hours, task completion, and subject focus with interactive charts.

- 📚 **Resource Organizer**  
  Upload, tag, and manage study notes and PDFs.

- 👥 **Collaboration (Optional)**  
  Share schedules and study sessions with friends for group study.

- ⚙️ **Profile & Settings**  
  Customize notifications, theme (Light/Dark), and personal info.

---

## 🌐 Website Pages

1. **Home / Landing Page** – Overview and CTA buttons.  
2. **Dashboard** – Calendar, tasks, and progress summary.  
3. **Schedule Planner** – Add courses, assignments, exams, and AI-generated schedules.  
4. **Focus / Timer** – Pomodoro timer with study session tracking.  
5. **Resources** – Upload and organize notes.  
6. **Analytics** – Interactive charts and graphs.  
7. **Profile / Settings** – Personal info, notifications, and theme selection.
